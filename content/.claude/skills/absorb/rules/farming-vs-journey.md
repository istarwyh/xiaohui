# Farming vs Journey Boundary

`content/Farming in the cyber world.md` and `content/journey.md` may reference the same facts, but they serve different reader intents.

## `Farming in the cyber world.md`

Answers:

> Who is Xiaohui, what has he done, and what can he offer?

Use it for:

- identity and current positioning
- credentials
- work experience
- project list
- open-source work
- shipped products
- honors and public recognition
- services, consulting, membership, or cooperation signals
- contact information

Write in:

- profile style
- resume style
- conversion style

Emphasize:

- facts
- results
- metrics
- role and responsibility
- capability proof

Example style:

> 我是百万 MAU 蚂蚁保产品「保险快查」的 Agent 应用负责人，负责将 AI Agent 能力落地到真实保险业务场景中。

## `journey.md`

Answers:

> Why did Xiaohui become this person, and how did his judgment evolve?

Use it for:

- growth stages
- turning points
- responsibility upgrades
- belief changes
- hard-won lessons
- intellectual evolution
- transitions between domains

Write in:

- timeline style
- narrative style
- reflective style

Emphasize:

- why the event mattered
- what changed after it
- what capability or belief was upgraded
- which deeper article records the experience

Example style:

> 2026 年，我从 Agent demo / 内部工具阶段进入真实流量产品阶段，开始面对百万 MAU、用户体验、风控、稳定性和业务转化共同作用下的 Agent 应用问题。「保险快查」让我意识到，Agent 的终局不是会回答，而是在真实业务约束下可信地完成任务。

## Same event, different surfaces

When the same event belongs to both pages:

1. Put factual proof in `Farming in the cyber world.md`.
2. Put growth meaning in `journey.md`.
3. Put detailed thinking in a normal article.
4. Cross-link them instead of duplicating paragraphs.

## Do not

- Do not turn `journey.md` into a resume or changelog.
- Do not duplicate `Farming in the cyber world.md` paragraphs in `journey.md`.
- Do not update `Farming in the cyber world.md` unless the input improves credibility, positioning, offer, or contact conversion.
- Do not add every small event to `journey.md`; only add growth-significant milestones.
