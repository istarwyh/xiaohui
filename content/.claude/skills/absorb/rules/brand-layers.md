# Brand Layers

Use this model for every absorption decision. A single input may belong to multiple layers.

## 1. Portfolio layer — "What I can do"

Use this layer when the input proves capability or credibility.

Examples:

- shipped products
- open-source projects
- products with metrics
- awards and public recognition
- talks, publications, demos
- consulting or service offers
- role titles and responsibility markers

Targets:

- `content/Farming in the cyber world.md`
- `content/aboutme/`
- `content/show/`
- `content/membership.md` when the offer/community/subscription model changes
- future `content/projects.md`
- project-specific articles

Writing style:

- concise profile/resume/conversion style
- emphasize facts, results, metrics, roles, and capabilities
- avoid over-selling; credibility comes from specificity

Purpose:

> Prove what Xiaohui can do.

## 2. Journey layer — "Why I became this person"

Use this layer when the input is a meaningful growth signal.

Examples:

- growth experiences
- career milestones
- turning points
- identity shifts
- responsibility upgrades
- hard-won lessons
- belief changes
- transitions between domains, such as engineering → AI Agent → product/system responsibility

Canonical file:

- `content/journey.md`

Writing style:

- narrative timeline style
- emphasize why the event mattered, what changed, and what deeper record supports it
- keep it selective and high-signal
- add concise milestones and link to deeper articles

Purpose:

> Explain why Xiaohui became this person.

## 3. Knowledge layer — "What I have learned and systematized"

Use this layer when the input is reusable knowledge.

Examples:

- technical notes
- frameworks and methods
- reading notes
- reflections
- domain knowledge
- practical lessons that can help future readers

Targets:

- `content/program/`
- `content/learning/`
- `content/java/`
- `content/society/`
- `content/life/`
- other domain directories

Writing style:

- thoughtful, structured, reusable
- preserve ideas and reasoning, not just facts
- use `[[wikilink]]` to connect related notes

Purpose:

> Preserve what Xiaohui knows.

## Multi-layer absorption

A successful Agent project may become:

- a detailed article in `program/llm/` (knowledge layer),
- a short milestone in `journey.md` (journey layer),
- and a project card in a portfolio page (portfolio layer).

When in doubt, put detailed substance in the knowledge layer, add only a concise growth marker to `journey.md`, and update portfolio/profile surfaces only when there is credible proof of capability.
